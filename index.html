01About

Research rigor, production instincts.

I'm a machine learning engineer who still thinks like a researcher. I care about the whole life of a model: framing the problem, building data that holds up, measuring what actually matters, and then making the system fast and cheap enough to serve real users.

Today I build multimodal grading systems at Coschool AI, where vision-language models evaluate handwritten answer scripts against rubrics. Before that, as an Applied Research Scientist at ServiceNow, I built REDACT, a 25-language PII benchmark accepted at EMNLP 2026, and a simulated ServiceNow environment for testing AI agents on IT incidents. Earlier I was the founding ML engineer at Alonzo AI, and I fine-tuned Whisper for Indian courtrooms at Adalat AI.

I studied Electronics & Communication Engineering at IIIT Hyderabad (B.Tech + MS by Research). My thesis asks when a signal's underlying structure changes, from channel codes all the way to text embeddings. That habit of looking for structure in noise still shapes how I debug models.

Vision-language models that grade handwritten answer scripts, from the detection model up to cost-tuned inference.

  • Question-level multimodal evaluation: built a handwritten-answer evaluation system with rubric-guided VLM scoring at 95% accuracy; evaluating directly on images cut inference latency and cost by 35%.

  • Worksheet segmentation service: fine-tuned YOLOv11 (nano & small) for label/separator detection (mAP50 0.975) and deployed a Kubernetes-hosted FastAPI service for VLM question matching, cross-page answer stitching and separator recovery.
  • Worksheet-level batched grading: graded every question in an answer script against its rubric in a single VLM call; benchmarked 8 GPT and 5 Claude models and reduced input tokens by ~75% and evaluation cost by ~47%, with no mis-mapped answers.
  • Off-topic detection: designed a Gaussian Mixture Model classifier that separates on- and off-topic chatbot queries with 95% accuracy.
